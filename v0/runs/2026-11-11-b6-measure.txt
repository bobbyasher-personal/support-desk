THE THREE ROT MECHANISMS, quantified on this trace:
1. Dead contracts: ~64% of step 1's desk is system + contracts. The
   refund-timing ticket needs 2 of the 4 tools; issue_refund (~338)
   and create_draft_reply (~206) are pure carrying cost until the
   facts exist - and create_draft_reply is meaningless before them.
2. The blob that never leaves: step 2's search result is ~2,900 chars
   (~720 tokens of JSON) and is re-billed on steps 3 and 4 - and a
   second copy rides into the reflection pass as evidence.
3. Linear growth: +164/+349/+280 per step on a FOUR-step job. On a
   15-step job the last step's desk is the sum of all 14 before it -
   the step-41 failure shape (the needed fact buried, the bill and
   the confusion both compounding).
