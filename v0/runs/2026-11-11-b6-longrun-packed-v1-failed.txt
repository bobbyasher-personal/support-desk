   create_draft_reply" - even after the draft was saved and that tool
   had left the desk. The model looped trying to obey an impossible
   instruction. Fix: the closing line is state-dependent ("The reply
   is DONE... do not save another draft").
3. The B2 no-progress brake only catches CONSECUTIVE repeats; the loop
   evaded it by alternating calls. Fix: two more brakes - a
   repeated-call rule (same tool+inputs a third time in a run stops
   it) and an error-streak rule (two tool errors in a row escalates).
