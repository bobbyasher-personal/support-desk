# Exchanges

## What can be exchanged

A customer may exchange an item for a different size or color of the same product within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

## How an exchange ships

The replacement ships when the carrier scans the returned item. A prepaid return label is provided.

## Price differences

If the replacement costs more, the customer pays the difference. If it costs less, the difference is refunded to the original payment method.
